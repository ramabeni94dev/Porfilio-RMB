## Design Context

### Users
Recruiters are the primary audience. They need a quick, credible view of Ramiro's experience, selected projects, technical depth, CV, and contact details.

### Brand Personality
Precise, credible, quietly ambitious.

### Aesthetic Direction
Use NexoWorks as the visual reference: charcoal, warm off-white, editorial typography, restrained green accents, and generous asymmetric spacing. Keep the portfolio sober and bilingual.

### Design Principles
1. Lead with Ramiro's profile and strongest work.
2. Describe concrete implementation, not generic skill claims.
3. Use hierarchy through type and spacing rather than repetitive bordered cards.
4. Make CV and contact links easy to find on every viewport.
5. Align dates and claims with the supplied CVs.
