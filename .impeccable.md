## Design Context

### Users
Recruiters are the primary audience. They need to understand Ramiro's experience, selected work, technical depth, CV, and contact details quickly. Prospective clients may also visit.

### Brand Personality
Precise, credible, quietly ambitious. The site should show concrete evidence instead of broad claims.

### Aesthetic Direction
Take cues from Ramiro's NexoWorks site: charcoal surfaces, warm off-white typography, editorial display type, restrained green accents, large type, and asymmetric spacing. Keep the presentation sober for recruiters. Support Spanish and English and respect reduced motion.

### Design Principles
1. Put the person's role, experience, and strongest work in the first two scrolls.
2. Explain projects through their problem and implementation, using real anonymized imagery where available.
3. Use typography and spacing for hierarchy; reserve framed surfaces for media.
4. Make CV, work, and contact actions obvious on desktop and mobile.
5. Align claims and dates with the supplied bilingual CVs.
